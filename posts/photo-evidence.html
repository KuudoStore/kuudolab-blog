Why Photo Evidence Matters in Construction Quality Inspections

A construction quality inspection is not just about identifying defects. It is also about keeping clear records of what was inspected.

Photographs play an important role in documenting workmanship, identifying defects and supporting inspection findings.

However, when photos are stored separately in a phone gallery, it can become difficult to identify which project, location or inspection item they belong to.

Keeping Inspection Records Organised

BinaSync Quality helps construction professionals organise inspection findings and photographic evidence in one Android app.

Inspectors can record observations, attach photographs and review inspection results without relying on separate paper forms and photo folders.

The goal is simple: make construction quality inspections easier to document and review.

BinaSync Quality is free, standalone and contains no ads.

Better inspection records support better quality management.
